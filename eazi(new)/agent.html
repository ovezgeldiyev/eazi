BIO

Francesca has been an Eazi agent since the launch of the agency in 2017. 

She is an absolute powerhouse of an estate agent with more than 18 years of experience and hundreds of successful transactions. She has been Eazi's number-one field agent for the past six years and is driven to get the best out of every sale for her clients. Before joining Eazi, Francesca was a top-performing agent with the Leapfrog Property Group.

Francesca is a field agent in the Southern Suburbs. As a resident agent in her area, she has an extensive understanding of the local market trends and prices and can accurately match buyers' property needs.
She works in a team with Christelle Soubirou, a full-status agent who has concluded hundreds of successful transactions with Eazi.

A fully qualified agent, she holds both her NQF 4 and NQF 5 in real estate as a Master Practitioner in Real Estate with the Property Practitioners Regulatory Authority.
